# Instagram content engine → quiz funnel

## Pillars

1. **Clarity over hype** — Explain mechanics in plain English (IUL caps/participation, annuity riders = generic education).
2. **California-aware** — Cost of living, business owners, equity comp families (no individualized advice).
3. **Community** — “Comment CLARITY” / poll stickers driving DM automation.

## Post ideas (feed)

- Carousel: “5 questions to ask before you fund permanent life insurance.”
- Reel: 45s on **protection vs. accumulation** framing — CTA: link in bio → funnel.
- Quote card: “We don’t optimize for hype — we optimize for fit.” → quiz.

## Stories

- Poll: “Tax clarity vs. retirement income clarity — which keeps you up?” → DM follow-up with funnel URL.
- Quiz sticker linking to `/quiz` URL (when Meta allows external links).

## Call-trigger posts

- Short hook: “If you’ve been considering indexed strategies, do this 2-minute check-in before you talk to anyone.”

## DM triggers

- Keyword **CLARITY** → auto-reply with funnel URL + expectation setting (see `docs/automation-workflows.md`).
- Story reply → tag `source:instagram_story_reply` in CRM.

## CTA angles (copy-safe)

- “Educational snapshot — not a quote.”
- “See how your priorities rank — then decide if a call is worth it.”
